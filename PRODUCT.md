# TraceLens — product context

## What it is
Open-source observability, tracing, and reliability platform for AI agents. Teams send traces of agent runs (LLM calls, tool calls, retrievals) via a Python SDK, framework integrations, or OpenTelemetry, then inspect failures, latency, tokens, and cost, investigate root causes, run evaluations, and version prompts.

## Audience and scene
Engineers who build and operate LLM agents, often while debugging a production failure: at a desk next to an IDE and terminal, frequently on call outside daylight hours, in long focused sessions. Dark-first follows from that scene.

## Surfaces and modes
- Dashboard, trace explorer, trace detail, evaluations, prompts, settings: **Operate**. Scanability and trust over expression.
- Landing page: **Persuade** for developers evaluating a self-hosted tool. Proof is the product itself.
- Docs: **Read**.

## Brand commitments (confirmed by the owner)
- Feel: precision instrument. Dense, calm, exact. Monospace only for data, IDs, code, and measurements.
- Accent: signal amber, reserved for primary actions, selection, and focus. Status colors (ok green, error red) stay distinct from it.
- Component family: shadcn-style on Radix primitives, Tailwind, Lucide icons.

## Truth constraints
- Never show fabricated metrics, customers, benchmarks, or integration claims. Empty states teach the next step instead of faking data.
- Integration status reflects what is implemented and tested in this repository.
- Costs are labeled as reported, calculated (from versioned list prices), partial, or unavailable.
